Why it matters

Paper records cost farmers money

Manual logbooks and verbal price deals leave production data incomplete and farmers underpaid — long after the harvest is already in.

Records get lost

Notebooks are damaged by weather and pests, and errors go unnoticed until reports are due.

Reporting lags behind

Technicians spend days consolidating scattered logs before anyone can see the real numbers.

Farmers underprice

Without market visibility or direct buyer access, traders set the terms — and the price.

What's inside

One system, from planting to sale

Eleven features split across two jobs: keeping production organized, and getting produce to buyers at a fair price.

Batch crop planner

Organize and schedule planting activities batch by batch.

Farm supply ledger

Low stock notification

Pest & disease outbreak alert

Predictive asset procurement

Sell the harvest

Market price fluctuation estimator

Price adjuster

B2B demand prediction

Anomaly detection pipeline

Flag unusual patterns in production or sales data for admin review.

How it works

Input, process, output

Farm data goes in, AgriTrack does the accounting, and farmers get records, alerts, and reports back out.

Input

  • Farmer personal and farm information
  • Crop planting schedules and batch data
  • Farm supply inventory data
  • Supply threshold settings
  • Pest and disease incident reports
  • Production cycle and asset data
→

Process

  • Farmer registration and validation
  • Batch crop planning and scheduling
  • Supply ledger recording and monitoring
  • Low stock detection and alert generation
  • Outbreak analysis and alert processing
  • Predictive asset procurement computation
→

Output

  • Farmer profile and account
  • Batch crop plans
  • Farm supply inventory records
  • Low stock notifications
  • Pest and disease alerts
  • Production and sales reports
